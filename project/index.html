We start where the crop starts.

Most buyers meet Indian produce three or four hands after the farm. By then the grade has drifted, the price has stacked up, and nobody can tell you which district it came from.

MOOLSORCE works the other way. We buy in the producing belt, close to the grower or the mill, and we put the origin on the paperwork. You get a lot you can trace and a price you can read.

How an order moves

Seven steps, inquiry to your dock.

Your contract carries the dates that bind.

Loading ports
Mundra · Nhava Sheva · Chennai · Kochi
Current focus
Gulf · Europe · Southeast Asia · East Africa
  1. 01

    Inquiry

    You send product, grade, quantity and destination port. We confirm what is available, and what is not, within the working day.

  2. 02

    Samples dispatched

    A 250 g to 1 kg sample per product goes by courier. We hold a sealed twin under the same lot number.

  3. 03

    Quotation and contract

    Written price with Incoterm, payment terms, packing and shipment window. Proforma invoice first, then the sales contract.

  4. 04

    Sourcing, quality check, packing

    We buy the lot, clean and grade it to the signed spec, test it, then pack and mark it to your labelling. Pre-shipment inspection by a third party if you ask for one.

  5. 05

    Documentation

    Draft documents come to you for approval before booking.

    • Commercial invoice
    • Packing list
    • Phytosanitary certificate
    • Certificate of origin
    • Lab report
    • Bill of lading
  6. 06

    Shipment from an Indian port

    Container stuffed under supervision, customs cleared, vessel loaded at Mundra, Nhava Sheva or Chennai, depending on product and your destination.

  7. 07

    Delivery

    You get tracking and the full document set per the agreed payment terms. Your agent clears at destination.
