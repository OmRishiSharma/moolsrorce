We start where the crop starts.

Most buyers meet Indian produce three or four hands after the farm. By then the grade has drifted, the price has stacked up, and nobody can tell you which district it came from.

MOOLSORCE works the other way. We buy in the producing belt, close to the grower or the mill, and we put the origin on the paperwork. You get a lot you can trace and a price you can read.

We are a small export desk. We would rather quote five products we can supply well than fifty we can only list.

How an order moves

Seven steps, inquiry to your dock.

Timings are typical, not promises. Your contract carries the dates that bind.

  1. 01

    Inquiry

    You send product, grade, quantity and destination port. We confirm what is available, and what is not, within the working day.

    Typical: Day 0 to 1

  2. 02

    Samples dispatched

    A 250 g to 1 kg sample per product goes by courier. We hold a sealed twin under the same lot number.

    Typical: Day 2 to 7

  3. 03

    Quotation and contract

    Written price with Incoterm, payment terms, packing and shipment window. Proforma invoice first, then the sales contract.

    Typical: Day 7 to 14

  4. 04

    Sourcing, quality check, packing

    We buy the lot, clean and grade it to the signed spec, test it, then pack and mark it to your labelling. Pre-shipment inspection by a third party if you ask for one.

    Typical: Day 14 to 28

  5. 05

    Documentation

    Draft documents come to you for approval before booking.

    • Commercial invoice
    • Packing list
    • Phytosanitary certificate
    • Certificate of origin
    • Lab report
    • Bill of lading
  6. 06

    Shipment from an Indian port

    Container stuffed under supervision, customs cleared, vessel loaded at Mundra, Nhava Sheva or Chennai, depending on product and your destination.

    Typical: Day 28 to 35

  7. 07

    Delivery

    You get tracking and the full document set per the agreed payment terms. Your agent clears at destination.

    Transit: by destination

Importer FAQ

Straight answers.

Not here? Write to the export desk and ask.

One 20 ft container for rice, pulses and onions. 1 tonne for spices, in 25 kg packs. Mixed-product containers are possible for spices and specialty items. Tell us your volume and we will tell you what is practical.

Samples up to 500 g per product are free. The buyer pays courier charges, or gives us a courier account number. Larger or pre-production samples are charged and credited against the first order.

Telegraphic transfer (TT) with an advance and the balance against copy documents, or an irrevocable letter of credit at sight from a bank we accept. Terms for repeat buyers are agreed case by case.

FOB (Indian port), CFR and CIF to your named port. If you want something else, ask. Each quote lists the cost lines separately.

Bulk: PP or jute bags of 25 to 50 kg, jumbo bags, cartons. Retail: pouches and boxes. Private label and printed bags are available. Print minimums and artwork specifications are confirmed with the quote.

Normally 3 to 5 weeks from confirmed order and advance to loading, depending on product, season and packing. Fresh produce follows the harvest and the shipping slot.

Phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and lab reports as standard. [List only what you hold: FSSAI, APEDA registration, ISO 22000, organic, HACCP.] Third-party inspection (SGS, Intertek or similar) on request, at the buyer's cost.
